# About

I am Ferran López Sierra, a mechanical engineer focused on computational methods, CAD/CAE workflows, numerical simulation, and engineering software.

My main interests are engineering software, CAE automation, hydraulic network modelling, geometry handling, and open-source workflows for analysis and documentation. I am especially interested in how programming can support modelling, preprocessing, solver interaction, validation, and clearer technical communication.

An international aeronautics-related internship experience at UNESP in Brazil, through IAESTE LC Lleida, also contributed to my interest in aerospace-oriented engineering environments, practical CAD/CAE work, and multidisciplinary technical contexts. It provided direct exposure to applied mechanical work linked to propeller blade development, classical mechanics, and numerical programming in an international setting.

This portfolio is used to document progressive technical development in a structured way. Some sections describe academic foundations, some represent active development, and others are clearly marked as learning or planned project lines.
